# Form 135 Field Reference

Form 135 (דוח שנתי מקוצר) is the short annual return for salaried employees claiming a tax refund.

## Key Fields

| Field # | Hebrew Label | English | Source |
|---------|-------------|---------|--------|
| 1 | ת.ז. | ID Number | Form 106 |
| 2 | שם מלא | Full Name | Form 106 |
| 3 | כתובת | Address | User input |
| 4 | שכר ברוטו | Gross Salary | Sum of all Form 106s |
| 5 | מס שנוכה | Tax Withheld | Sum of all Form 106s |
| 6 | ביטוח לאומי | Bituach Leumi | Sum of all Form 106s |
| 7 | מס בריאות | Health Tax | Sum of all Form 106s |
| 8 | הפקדות פנסיה | Pension Deposits | Form 106 + pension statements |
| 9 | תרומות סעיף 46 | Section 46 Donations | Donation receipts |
| 10 | נקודות זיכוי | Credit Points | Calculated from personal details |

## SHAAM Portal Notes

- Form 135 is under: שע"מ > דוחות > דוח שנתי מקוצר
- Tax year selector must be set before filling fields
- Some fields auto-calculate (greyed out) — do not try to fill those
- The portal may pre-populate data from employer submissions (Form 126)
